  • Slide the red and green levers for the engines; hold AHD or AST for a gentle bump. All stop brings both to neutral.
  • Hold ◀ or ▶ for the bow thruster.
  • One finger on the water orbits the camera; two fingers twist and pinch.
  • Tap Anchors, Lines or Fenders to open those controls. Tap the same heading again to close them.
  • Tap a line on the Lines panel to throw it, then hold Heave in or Slack. They work every line unless you tap one to single it out.

Harbour navigation

  • Follow the amber waypoint through clear water, then approach the berth slowly. In this harbour, aim for 6 kn or less near docks and in the channel.
  • Entering a marked channel, keep red marks to port and green marks to starboard. Keep to the starboard side when there is room.
  • An east cardinal mark has black and yellow bands and two outward-pointing cones. Pass to its east.
  • Meeting head-on: both boats turn to starboard. Crossing: give way to a boat on your starboard side and pass behind it.
  • Overtaking: keep clear until safely past. Leave room for slowing and turning, and act early if another boat is not keeping clear.
  • All stop selects neutral; momentum remains. Use short astern bursts to brake, and rig fenders before coming alongside.
  • At the final anchorage, drop inside the ring, pay out the chain shown in the objective, then hold the chain and settle for three seconds. The mission finishes with a camera pan and zoom.
